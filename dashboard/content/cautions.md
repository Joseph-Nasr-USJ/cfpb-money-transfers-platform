## Cautions shown next to each result

One line per chart, to be shown directly under the chart title or caption.

**Q1. Growth of sub-products and issues, and the mobile or digital wallet share**
Growth in complaints is not growth in usage or harm; the current year is incomplete; a trend is not a forecast.

**Q2. Fraud-or-scam share across the four core sub-products**
The issue is chosen by the consumer; it is not verified fraud.

**Q3. Mix of submission channels over time**
A change in channel mix may reflect how complaints were routed or collected, not what consumers prefer.

**Q4. Response categories and timely-response rates, by sub-product and company**
Shares of complaints only: there is no market-share denominator, so this is not a ranking of company quality.

**Q5. Issue profiles by state**
Population is not the user base; states with no recorded value are shown as *Unknown*; groups describe complaint profiles, not consumers.

**Emerging issues (Spark MLlib)**
A rising share of published complaints is not proof of rising fraud, harm or usage. It is a trend in past data, not a forecast.

**State groups (Spark MLlib)**
Groups describe complaint profiles, not consumers, companies or service quality. States with fewer than 100 complaints are not grouped.

**Processing lag chart**
This is the time until the CFPB sent the complaint to the company, not the company's resolution time.

**Data quality and freshness**
The quality report describes the last run only. A successful load does not mean the newest complaints are included: check the latest complaint date.
