## About this dashboard

This dashboard shows complaints about **money transfers and digital payments**: domestic and international money transfers, mobile or digital wallets and virtual currency. It is for policy analysts and service or operations managers.

**Data source.** The public Consumer Complaint Database of the US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB): the full historical file plus daily updates from the CFPB API.

**Scope.** Complaints in this domain, counted once by complaint ID and dated by the day the CFPB received them. Use the filters to select a period, sub-product, issue, state, submission channel or company.

**Updates.** New complaints are collected every 24 hours. The freshness indicator shows the latest complaint date and the last successful load. The most recent weeks are provisional.

**Read with care.** These are published complaints, not all customers or transactions. The analysis describes what was reported; it does not explain causes or predict. See *Limitations*.
