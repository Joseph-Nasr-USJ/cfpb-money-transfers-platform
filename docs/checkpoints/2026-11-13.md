## Completed risk-reduction action

The official `minio/minio` image can no longer be pulled (MinIO stopped publishing official images in October 2025), which would have blocked the whole stack on every machine. It was replaced by `alpine/minio:RELEASE.2025-10-15T17-29-55Z`, a community rebuild of the last release, pinned to its exact tag, with ports bound to `127.0.0.1` (defect D-001, D-002). Pinning the tag makes every team member start the same version. The image build was also made resumable after two download failures on slow connections (D-003, D-004).
