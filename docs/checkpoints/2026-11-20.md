## Completed risk-reduction action

The data-quality rules and their thresholds (uniqueness, completeness, validity, consistency, freshness, reconciliation) were fixed in code before any real result existed, and a deliberate set of bad records (an invalid date, an invalid state, a negative receipt-to-referral interval, a missing Complaint ID) was shown to be caught by the intended checks (defect D-005). This removes the risk of tuning the thresholds to the results, and shows that bad data cannot reach MongoDB silently.
